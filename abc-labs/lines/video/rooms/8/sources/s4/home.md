# Cardboard: the agentic AI video editor for your browser and Mac

Cardboard is an agentic video editor that runs in the browser and as a free Mac app. Describe the edit you want and Cardboard cuts, captions, reframes, and composes it. Backed by Y Combinator.

## Drive the edit

- **Natural language editing.** Describe the edit ("cut the awkward pauses", "make this look more cinematic") and Cardboard performs the precise timeline operations.
- **Browser-native rendering.** A custom WebGL2 compositor and a WebCodecs decoder do the work locally. On the web there is no software to install.
- **Mac app.** Cardboard for Mac (Apple Silicon) is free to download with the Lite plan included, keeps your footage on your Mac, and lets you edit with your own Claude Code or Codex.
- **Multi-layer timeline.** Video, audio, image, text, and caption tracks with drag-and-drop editing and keyframes.
- **Live collaboration.** Review, comment on, tweak, and ship an edit together.

## Cut and assemble

- **Transcript-first cutting.** Remove bad takes, false starts, fillers, stutters, and repetitions from spoken footage while the meaning survives.
- **Silence and gap removal.** Detect dead air, cut it, and close the gaps it leaves behind.
- **Highlight extraction.** Surface the moments worth keeping from long recordings and cut them into a recap.
- **Beat-synced montages.** Compile highlight reels and travel or event montages with cuts landing on the music.
- **Multicam sync.** Align several camera angles of one session by their audio and cut between speakers.
- **Transitions.** Place visual transitions between clips and smooth the cut points.
- **Batch cleanup.** Apply one cleanup pass across every clip in the project with consistent settings.

## Find and source footage

- **Scene and speech search.** Find footage by describing what happened or what was said, not by filename.
- **Stock B-roll.** Drop stock B-roll on the exact moment each concept is spoken, sized to how long it stays the subject.
- **Archival documentary sourcing.** Cover a narrated documentary or origin story with real archival footage of the subject from public archives.

## Frame and finish

- **Reframe and speaker tracking.** Recompose an edit for vertical or square, and punch in on whoever is speaking across split, grid, hero, and PIP layouts.
- **Framing repair.** Diagnose and remove black bars, letterboxing, and exposed canvas around a clip.
- **Colour grading and filters.** Cinematic looks, LUTs, and lift/gamma/gain colour wheels applied consistently across the timeline.
- **Background removal.** Remove or replace the background on video and image clips.
- **Depth compositing.** Layer titles, captions, and logos behind the subject rather than flat on top of the frame.
- **Keyframe animation.** Ken Burns moves, zooms, pans, fades, and any property animated over time.
- **Visual review.** Grade the finished timeline on shot selection, typography, colour, composition, text placement, and pacing.

## Words and sound

- **Auto captions.** Word-level captions in seconds, with customisable presets, per-caption editing, and translation into other languages.
- **Voiceover generation.** AI narrator voiceovers from a script, and edits built around the narration timing.
- **Music and sound design.** Generate original background music and sound effects, then mix them under dialogue with a sane volume hierarchy.
- **Voice dubbing.** Dub a video into another language with an AI voice track.
- **Audio cleanup.** Duck the music under speech, level volume across the edit, and strip background noise.
- **Reference matching.** Match the typography or reuse the audio from a reference image or video attached in chat.

## Formats and delivery

- **Platform presets.** Format, pacing, and caption constraints for TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, YouTube, and LinkedIn.
- **Video formats.** Purpose-built workflows for explainers, tutorials, product demos, and vlogs.
- **Export.** Render in the browser or on the server, or export an AAF for Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, and Avid Media Composer.
