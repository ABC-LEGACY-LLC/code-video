# Cardboard pricing

Cardboard plans are credit-metered: usage credits cover the agent, such as analysis, captions, voiceover, and server-side exports, and generative credits cover AI video and image generation.

## Lite

- Monthly: $8/month
- First month for new subscribers: $5
- Annual: $96/year ($8/month)
- Maximum upload size: 2.5 GB per file
- Projects: unlimited

## Starter

- Monthly: $29/month
- First month for new subscribers: $9
- Annual: $240/year ($20/month)
- Maximum upload size: 2.5 GB per file
- Projects: unlimited

## Creator

- Monthly: $59/month
- First month for new subscribers: $30
- Annual: $468/year ($39/month)
- Maximum upload size: 2.5 GB per file
- Projects: unlimited

## Pro

- Monthly: $175/month
- First month for new subscribers: $89
- Annual: $1500/year ($125/month)
- Maximum upload size: 10 GB per file
- Projects: unlimited

## Notes

- Prices are in US dollars and exclude any tax collected at checkout.
- Lite is free in the Cardboard Mac app and a paid plan on the web.
- Current plans and checkout: https://www.cardboard.ai/pricing
