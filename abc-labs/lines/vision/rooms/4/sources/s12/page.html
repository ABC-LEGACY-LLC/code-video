Apronai

Get real-time visibility & predictive alerting for improved turnaround performance

ApronAI enhances airport, airline, and ground handler operations through real-time visibility of turnaround operations. With strategically placed cameras across aircraft stands, aerobridges, and the apron, accurate timestamps for turnaround events are generated in real time. Our advanced machine-learning techniques enable accurate predictions for aircraft off-block (POBT) and departure readiness (PRDT).

Resource Management

Get accurate, real-time predictions for when aircraft will leave the gate and be ready for takeoff. Our advanced system uses live turnaround data, flight details, weather updates, and more to provide precise off-block times (POBT) and ready times (PRDT) - consistently beating current alternatives.

These predictions make resource management a whole lot easier. Airports and airlines can efficiently allocate gates, pushback tugs, and loading teams without unnecessary delays. By increasing predictability, this system minimizes the need for excessive planning buffers, streamlining operations and saving time.
