The Stand Visibility Gap: Why Turnaround Milestone Data Can Be Inconsistent

In many airport environments, turnaround milestones are confirmed manually, estimated from experience, or recorded retrospectively across separate systems, introducing variability that directly affects departure predictability.

This limits shared visibility across airlines, ground handlers, airport operations, and air traffic control. As a result, Predicted Off-Block Time (POBT) can drift from actual aircraft readiness, while emerging bottlenecks remain invisible until they begin to impact departure sequencing.

As traffic volumes increase and operational buffers narrow, even minor inconsistencies at the stand can affect overall departure performance. Addressing this requires a shift from estimated, manually confirmed milestones to objective, real-time visibility of turnaround activity.

How Smart Stand Converts Stand Activity into Departure Intelligence

Real-time airfield visibility, performance monitoring, predictive insights, and historical analysis in one shared workspace.

Smart Stand addresses this operational gap by introducing AI-enabled identification and tracking of turnaround activity directly at the stand, generating structured, real-time operational data to support coordinated departure readiness.

Smart Stand continuously monitors turnaround activity at the stand using live camera feeds and AI. AI automatically identifies and timestamps operational milestones such as aircraft on- and off-block, bridge connection, fueling activity, baggage handling progression, and pushback readiness.

Smart Stand integrates directly into your existing AODB, AOP, and A-CDM systems, so milestone data reaches the right teams and systems, keeping all stakeholders aligned in real time.
