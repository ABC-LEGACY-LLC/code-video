This limits shared visibility across airlines, ground handlers, airport operations, and air traffic control. As a result, Predicted Off-Block Time (POBT) can drift from actual aircraft readiness, while emerging bottlenecks remain invisible until they begin to impact departure sequencing. As traffic volumes increase and operational buffers narrow, even minor inconsistencies at the stand can affect overall departure performance. Addressing this requires a shift from estimated, manually confirmed milestones to objective, real-time visibility of turnaround activity. How Smart Stand Converts Stand Activity into Departure Intelligence Real-time airfield visibility, performance monitoring, predictive insights, and historical analysis in one shared workspace. Smart Stand Frequently Asked Questions What is Smart Stand? + Smart Stand is Searidge&#8217;s AI-powered turnaround intelligence solution. It uses AI-driven computer vision to detect and timestamp the key milestones of each aircraft turnaround, turning live stand activity into structured operational data. That data flows into real-time dashboards, alerts, milestone timelines, and synchronized playback, giving teams a shared, live view of turnaround progress and departure readiness across the gate network. The result is more predictable departures, stronger coordination, and better stand performance. How does Smart Stand work? + Smart Stand processes live video feeds from stand-mounted cameras using Searidge&#8217;s AI engine, which automatically detects and timestamps turnaround activity in real time. Detected events are integrated into your operational systems including AODB, AOP, and A-CDM. Smart Stand can be deployed on-premise, in the cloud, or in a hybrid configuration depending on your airport&#8217;s IT and operational requirements. What turnaround milestones does Smart Stand detect? + Smart Stand automatically detects and timestamps a range of critical turnaround milestones including aircraft on-block and off-block, chocks placement and removal, bridge connection, fuelling activity, baggage handling milestones, door status, pushback initiation, and safety-related stand conditions including wing walker and marshaller positioning, ground service equipment placement, and stand zone compliance. Smart Stand can also incorporate surrounding apron context that may influence turnaround progression, including ground service vehicle movement, congestion near the stand, and access constraints impacting pushback readiness. What systems does Smart Stand integrate with? + Smart Stand integrates with AODB, AOP, and A-CDM systems through Chorus. Turnaround data flows automatically into these systems, reducing manual re-entry and keeping every stakeholder aligned in real time, without requiring a separate interface. How does Smart Stand support post-event review and operational disputes? + Smart Stand records stand video and supports synchronized, time-aligned playback of every turnaround.
