# s15 — Farmers Weekly, 3 March 2023, on CattleEye (camera lameness detection)

Read 2026-09-30. Near-verbatim through a summarising fetch.

- "a low-cost camera mounted over the exit of the parlour which captures footage of each animal as it
  leaves" and "an artificial intelligence algorithm to pick out key points on each cow and assess its gait"
- Needs "electronic identification (EID)"; the camera "4m above the ground" with 4 m of unobstructed walk.
- "CattleEye costs about £1 a cow a month, reducing to 75p a cow a month for larger herds. The set-up
  costs are about £500 for the camera and equipment."

Bearing: lameness from key points on video is a sold product — from a fixed camera over a race every cow
walks through daily, with EID; not from a pasture camera in one second [s2].
