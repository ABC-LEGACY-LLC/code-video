# s9 — Andrew et al., "Visual Identification of Individual Holstein-Friesian Cattle via Deep Metric Learning" (arXiv 2006.09205)

Read 2026-09-30 (abstract page). Submitted 16 June 2020, final version 14 October 2020.
Authors: William Andrew, Jing Gao, Siobhan Mullan, Neill Campbell, Andrew W Dowsey, Tilo Burghardt.

- Holstein-Friesians have "individually-characteristic black and white coat patterns" which the work
  uses to identify individuals from overhead images, without tags.
- "93.8% accuracy when trained on just half of the population"

Bearing: tagless visual ID works where the coat carries a pattern. Compare [s10] for solid black cattle.
