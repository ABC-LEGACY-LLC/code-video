Free image-to-3D models don't preserve your source image - text, logos and fine detail come back garbled. 
I got that working with my latest 'Pixel Match' feature in my image-to-3dlab: it copies the actual pixels from your source onto the model, even after retopo down to ~5k faces. High quality, low poly 3D models that are riggable and can be animated. 
One step closer to Tripo / Meshy level of output, but free and on your own machine. Mac + NVIDIA. 
Try it out here: https://github.com/Bingeljell/image-to-3dlab

--- 2104542160079270161 @Bingeljell Mon Sep 28 12:02:06 +0000 2026 replies=1 likes=5 views=428
Image to 3dlab has some nice QoL updates out now, with a big feature coming up tomorrow. 

The QoL updates:
1. Standardised the bg removal with BiRefNet-lite. It's amazing at getting rid of backgrounds and is lightning fast. ~1 sec to load and ~4 sec for bg removal on my mac.
2. Pixal3D runs only builds a 1024 res model - but complex and 'chunky' images can take more time. This isn't an issue on Nvidia cards, but for Apple Silicon users, this can increase time taken to generate a good 3D model. Have optimised steps from 12>8 - no visible loss in quality. Works out of the box for fresh installs. Existing users will have to run a script to rebuild from source. 
3. Minor fixes to how the viewer was loading up in the Generate 3D tab. Cleaner now. And images from Qwen now land in the /output/images/ folder - better organised for your viewing pleasure. 

Download the latest version here: https://github.com/Bingeljell/image-to-3dlab 

We have a really nice upgrade for retopology and new photo layer /paint step to keep the assets looking good even as low as ~5k faces. Also a much better user experience across the web viewer that should make life easy for game devs - Coming up in tomorrow's release. So stay tuned!
