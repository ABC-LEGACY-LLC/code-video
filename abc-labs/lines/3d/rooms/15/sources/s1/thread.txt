Free image-to-3D models don't preserve your source image - text, logos and fine detail come back garbled. 
I got that working with my latest 'Pixel Match' feature in my image-to-3dlab: it copies the actual pixels from your source onto the model, even after retopo down to ~5k faces. High quality, low poly 3D models that are riggable and can be animated. 
One step closer to Tripo / Meshy level of output, but free and on your own machine. Mac + NVIDIA. 
Try it out here: https://github.com/Bingeljell/image-to-3dlab

There's lots more to do, so please test, raise issues & PRs, etc.
